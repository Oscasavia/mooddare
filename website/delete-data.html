Delete your account or data

MoodDare · Updated October 2, 2026

You can request deletion without installing or signing in to MoodDare. Choose whether to delete your entire account or only specific data.

Delete your MoodDare account

Email oscasavia@gmail.com with the subject MoodDare account deletion. Include your MoodDare username and the email associated with your account. Where possible, send the request from that email address. We may ask you to confirm account ownership before deleting anything. Never send your password or sign-in codes.

The MoodDare team handles emailed requests manually and will reply about verification and completion. Clicking the email link opens a draft; send the email to submit your request. If your email app does not open, copy the address above into your email service.

You can also request deletion in the app: Profile → Settings → Delete account. Complete the confirmation and any sign-in verification shown.

Account deletion removes your sign-in account, profile and avatar, uploaded posts and media, comments and replies, likes, follow and block relationships, saved dares, dare invitations, notification records and device registrations, and personalized-feed history. Your account will no longer be available. Replies by other people in threads you started may also be removed.

Delete data and keep your account

Email oscasavia@gmail.com with the subject MoodDare data deletion. Include your username, account email, and the specific posts, comments, replies or personalized-feed history you want deleted. Say that you want to keep your account. We may verify ownership and clarify which items you mean before processing the request.

In the app, you can delete your own posts, comments and replies through their three-dot menus. To delete recommendation history, open the Moments filter menu and choose Reset feed preferences. This keeps your account, likes, follows and saved dares. Recent Find people searches are stored on your device; clear them on that screen.

When you request deletion of selected data, your account and other data remain unless you also ask to delete them. Deleting a post also removes its associated discussion.

Timing and retained copies

After an account-deletion request is verified and accepted, server cleanup continues and retries temporary failures. Completion can take longer if a service is unavailable. Emailed requests require manual review before cleanup begins.

Cloud Storage retains recoverable soft-deleted media for its configured seven-day recovery period. Service-provider logs and backups may persist under their own retention schedules; those periods vary by service. Information may also be retained when legally required. We will explain any applicable retention when responding to your request.

Copies saved or shared by other users are outside our control. Local cached files may remain until cleared by the app, operating system or uninstalling the app. Posts leaving Moments after 24 hours are not deleted; they remain on profiles until deletion.

For more details, read our Privacy Policy.
