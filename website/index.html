A FEW LITTLE ANSWERS

Curious?
Good.

What is MoodDare?+

MoodDare is an app for mood-based challenges and shared moments. Choose how you feel, try a dare, and capture a photo or video. You can share it, meet people and join the conversation.

Can I download it yet?+

Public download links are coming soon. The Android and iOS buttons below will become live when the builds are ready to share publicly.

Do I have to share every dare?+

No. Sharing is optional, and every dare is an invitation. Skip anything that isn’t right for you. If you post a moment, other members can view, share and download it.

What happens after 24 hours?+

Moments leave the feed after 24 hours. Your own posts remain on your profile until you delete them. Leaving the feed doesn’t delete the media or copies other people have saved.

What are Daring and Epic?+

They’re upcoming premium mood collections. For now, they appear as locked previews in the app. No subscriptions are available through this site.
