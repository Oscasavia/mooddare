`google-g.png` is Google's unmodified official multicolor sign-in logo, downloaded from https://developers.google.com/static/identity/images/g-logo.png on 2026-09-19. Used only for “Continue with Google,” preserving its colors and proportions on a white button.

`mooddare-wordmark.png` is the approved custom lettering from
`design/branding/mooddare-v1/wordmark-dark.png`, created with built-in image
generation on 2026-09-22. The original PNG is preserved unchanged. The shared
`MoodDareWordmark` widget removes its dark presentation background at paint time
and renders the lettering in the app's lavender (#C5B4FF). It is used on Moments
and Welcome, with an accessible MoodDare heading label. The source prompts and
concept board are saved in the design folder. App-icon concepts are not installed.
